Delete your account and cloud data

This page explains how to request deletion of your Photo Note cloud account and the data associated with it.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Photo Note.
  2. Open the Cloud Backup screen.
  3. Select Delete cloud account and data.
  4. Review the warning and confirm deletion.

Request deletion without the app

If you have uninstalled Photo Note or cannot access the app, send an email with the subject “Photo Note Account Deletion.” Include the Google account email address that you used for Cloud Backup.

Request deletion by email

Contact email: nugabaa876@gmail.com

Data that will be deleted

  • Your Firebase account used by Photo Note
  • Cloud-backed note text and tags
  • Photos uploaded to Cloud Backup
  • Cloud Backup usage information associated with your account

Data not controlled by the service provider

Notes and photos stored only on your device are not accessible to the service provider. Remove them in the app or through your device’s standard uninstall process.

Google Play may retain purchase records according to Google’s legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, and regulatory obligations.

Processing time

Verified deletion requests will normally be completed within 30 days. Reasonable verification may be requested to prevent unauthorized deletion of another person’s account.

Deleting a Photo Note cloud account is permanent and cannot be undone.
